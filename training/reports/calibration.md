Temperatures are fitted on dev (NLL); ECE is top-1 expected calibration error (15 bins). Margins are chosen on dev: most errors recovered (gold label or PP head offered as an alternative) with mean alternatives per sentence ≤ 0.3 (cap 5; proposed rule, owner-set mean cap). The role threshold (T59) is the lowest role confidence (relation × head probability of the role's head word) whose dev role precision is ≥ the same target, with brackets gated at the bracket threshold and roles of collapsed brackets shown as head-word badges.
