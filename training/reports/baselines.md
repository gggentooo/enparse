## compromise tag mapping

Tokens without letters or digits → PUNCT (SYM for `$ % & + < = > @ # ^ | ~ € £ ¥`), whatever compromise tagged them. `Conjunction` → CCONJ for `and`, `or`, `but`, `nor`, `yet`, `&`, `plus`, PART for `to` (compromise's infinitive marker), else SCONJ. Then the first matching compromise tag wins: `Pronoun`→PRON, `ProperNoun`→PROPN, `Determiner`→DET, `Preposition`→ADP, `Negative`→PART, `Modal`→AUX, `Auxiliary`→AUX, `Copula`→AUX, `Verb`→VERB, `Adjective`→ADJ, `Adverb`→ADV, `Value`→NUM, `Expression`→INTJ, `QuestionWord`→PRON, `Noun`→NOUN; anything else → X. Chunks: compromise `Noun`→NP, `Verb`→VP, `Adjective`→ADJP; prepositions → single-word PP; punctuation, conjunctions, symbols → O.
